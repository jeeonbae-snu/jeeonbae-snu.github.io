About Me

Machine learning for practical autonomy.

Hi, I am Je-Eon Bae, a machine learning engineer and first-year master's student in Future Automotive Mobility Engineering at Seoul National University. I am currently affiliated with the Artificial Intelligence & Autonomous Driving Laboratory .

My work is motivated by a simple goal: make machine learning systems more reliable, efficient, and valuable in real-world settings. I am especially interested in vision-language-action models, autonomous mobility, data-efficient learning, model compression, and semi-supervised learning.
